# 替换稿件邮件模板（发给 Physica Scripta 编辑部）

## 发送方式（按推荐顺序）

1. **直接回复投稿确认邮件**（最稳）——ScholarOne 提交后会给你发确认邮件
   （"Submission Confirmation"），直接点"回复"，把下面正文粘贴进去。这样
   自动带上稿件编号，也一定能到达编辑部。
2. 或用 ScholarOne 稿件详情页里的 "Send E-mail" / "Contact Journal" 功能（如有）
3. IOP 官网 Physica Scripta 的 Contact/Editorial office 页面查到的编辑部邮箱

## 邮件正文（英文，直接复制）

**Subject:** Manuscript [请填稿件编号] – Request to replace manuscript file
(correction to data availability statement)

---

Dear Editor,

I am writing regarding my manuscript submitted to Physica Scripta on
[提交日期，如 10 September 2026]:

"What do spectral criteria of quantum feature-map kernels actually predict?
Classical simulability, generalization advantage, and the role of task
difficulty" (Manuscript ID: [请填编号]).

I would like to request replacing the submitted PDF with a corrected version.
The data availability statement in the submitted file incorrectly stated that
the code and data are included as arXiv ancillary files. In fact, I have not
posted an arXiv preprint; the code and data are openly available at
https://github.com/python123flask/quantum-kernel-spectral, as correctly
indicated by the URL in the same statement. No other changes have been made:
the science, results, figures, and conclusions are identical.

The corrected PDF is attached. I would be grateful if you could replace the
file, or advise me if there is a preferred procedure.

I apologize for the oversight and thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,
Xianzhe Liu
School of Physics and Electronics, Hunan University, Changsha 410082, China
Email: liuxianzhe@hnu.edu.cn

---

## 中文对照（方便你确认意思）

- 核心诉求：我提交的稿件里"数据可用性声明"有一处表述有误——写了"代码和数据
  作为 arXiv 附件随稿提供"，但实际上我没有发布 arXiv 预印本；代码和数据公开
  在 GitHub（链接在声明里已正确给出）。请求用修正确认版替换文件。
- 强调：**科学内容、结果、图、结论完全没变**（这点很重要，避免编辑误解为大改动）。
- 礼貌收尾 + 道歉 + 感谢。

## 提示

- [括号]里的内容记得替换成你的稿件编号和提交日期（编号在确认邮件里）
- 发送后如收到编辑回复（同意替换或给出上传方式），按指示操作即可
- 若编辑要求上传修正版：在 ScholarOne 里通常可以用 "Replace File" 或
  重新提交修订版（Revision），按编辑部指示做
